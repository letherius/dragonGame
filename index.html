ADVENTURER'S GUIDE

How to survive Emberfall

01 Explore

Visit the store and cave to gather equipment, gold, and experience.

02 Grow stronger

Defeat monsters to gain XP. Leveling increases your maximum health.

03 Upgrade

Better weapons deal more damage. Armor reduces incoming monster attacks.

04 Slay the dragon

Reach level 4 and obtain a worthy weapon before entering the final battle.

Combat moves

Attack is dependable. Power Strike deals heavier damage but may miss. Dodge attempts to evade and counter. Potion heals you but gives the enemy a turn.
